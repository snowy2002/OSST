C=70, L0=1: rigorous finite-n upper-tail certificate, 75 <= n <= 403

Scope and conclusion
--------------------
For every integer 75 <= n <= 403, every integer 1 <= S <= n, every
collection of independent Poisson variables N_i with arbitrary means
lambda_i >= 0, and the unchanged multiscale estimator coefficients,

  P[there exists j: Z_j > (1+delta_j) S + delta_j n]
    < 0.291303649639558466 < 0.32.

The bound is uniform in the Poisson means, so in particular it holds when
lambda_i=m p_i with the algorithm's real mean m. The numeric maximum over
the certified integer range occurs at n=75. This result controls the upper
deviation only. It does not cover n<75 or n>403, and it does not by itself
prove the full estimator theorem.

Moment generating function
--------------------------
At scale j let k=k_j, Y=f_j(Poi(lambda))-1, y_0=-1, and
y_l=f_j(l)-1 for 1 <= l <= k. For l>k the correction is zero. Thus

  G_j(t,lambda) = E exp(t Y)
               = 1 + exp(-lambda) sum_{l=0}^k
                         [exp(t y_l)-1] lambda^l/l!.

The negative zero-count term exp(-lambda)[exp(-t)-1] is retained exactly.
Let t>0 and eta>0 be the rational certificate values. The checker proves
G_j(t,lambda) <= 1+eta for every lambda>=0 as follows.

Polynomial certificate
----------------------
With d=max(k,48), define

  P(x)=eta sum_{l=0}^d x^l/l!
       -sum_{l=0}^k [exp(t y_l)-1] x^l/l!.

Since exp(x)>=sum_{l=0}^d x^l/l! for x>=0, nonnegativity of P(x) implies
eta exp(x)>=sum_{l=0}^k [exp(t y_l)-1]x^l/l!, hence the claimed MGF bound.
The checker uses 512-bit outward Arb intervals for every coefficient.

On [0,R] with R=8, it recursively bisects intervals [a,b] until every
Bernstein coefficient of P(a+(b-a)u), 0<=u<=1, has strictly positive
interval lower bound. Nonnegative Bernstein basis functions sum to one,
so each such interval has P>0. The saved leaf intervals form a complete
binary partition of [0,R].

For x>=R the checker expands P(R+u) and verifies every coefficient has
nonnegative interval lower bound. Therefore P(R+u)>=0 for every u>=0.
This covers the entire half-line; no finite sampling grid is used as proof.

The power-to-Bernstein conversion is itself carried out by outward Arb
polynomial arithmetic. If q(u)=sum q_j u^j has degree at most d, then

  beta_i = (i!/d!) sum_{j=0}^i (d-j)! q_j/(i-j)!.

Chernoff and support-size endpoint reduction
-------------------------------------------
Independence yields E exp(t sum_i Y_i) <= (1+eta)^S. Consequently

  P[Z_j>(1+delta_j)S+delta_j n]
    <= exp[S log(1+eta)-t delta_j(n+S)].

The exponent is affine in S. Over every integer 1<=S<=n it is bounded by
the larger of its two endpoint values,

  max{log(1+eta)-t delta_j(n+1),
      n log(1+eta)-2t delta_j n}.

The verifier sums these exponential bounds over all active scales. It
checks that the resulting Arb upper interval is less than 8/25 for each
of the 329 integers, covering 2570 scale certificates. The observed-count
term D cannot exceed S, so it does not create an additional upper event.

Reproduction
------------
cd /home/snowy/OSST
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 .venv/bin/python proof_l01/mgf.py --start 75 --end 403
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 .venv/bin/python proof_l01/mgf.py --certify

The first command is nonrigorous optimization used only to find candidate
rational t and eta values. The second independently checks all inequalities
required above. A poor optimizer proposal causes certificate failure; it
cannot silently establish a false proof claim. A 0.2% eta margin and upward
rational rounding are checked rather than assumed sufficient.
