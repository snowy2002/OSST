C0 = 1025: concise proof note
===========================

Build from /home/snowy/OSST:

Alternatively, run pdflatex on c0_1025_note.tex twice in its directory.
Standard packages: geometry, fontenc, lmodern, amsmath, amssymb,
amsthm, mathtools, microtype, hyperref, enumitem. No BibTeX step is needed.
Tectonic downloads its TeX resources on the first build.

Scope
  Standalone theoretical note suitable for adaptation into a paper appendix.
  No particular conference template or submission track was specified.
  The result concerns Wang's unchanged construction, L0 = 1024, and a
  per-promise error target below 1 percent. Integer optimality does not
  assert minimax optimality across algorithms or optimality over real C0.
  Epsilon is the distribution-distance parameter, not the failure rate.
